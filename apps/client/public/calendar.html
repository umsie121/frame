Connect iCloud Calendars

All calendars on your Apple account will appear on the family frame. It can only read them.

  1. Open account.apple.com and sign in.
  2. Go to Sign-In and Security → App-Specific Passwords, tap +, name it “Family Frame”.
  3. Copy the password it shows (looks like abcd-efgh-ijkl-mnop) and paste it below.
Use an app-specific password, not your normal Apple ID password. You can revoke it any time at account.apple.com and the frame will simply stop syncing.
