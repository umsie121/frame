Google Photos

Photos added to a shared Google Photos album will appear on the family frame by themselves, from anywhere.

  1. In Google Photos, create an album (e.g. “Family Frame”).
  2. Tap Share → Create link (link sharing on) and copy the link.
  3. Paste it below. Invite family to the album so they can add photos.
The frame checks the album every 20 minutes. No Google account or password is stored on it. Anyone with the album link can view the album, so share it only with family.
